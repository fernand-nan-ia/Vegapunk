## Resumo

Página canônica de Lilith, a Punk-02, Satélite que encarna a faceta do Mal de Vegapunk e a única Vegapunk que escapou de Egghead com os Chapéus de Palha. Mulher alta de cabelo ruivo selvagem cobrindo o olho direito e olhos roxos; usa macacão rosa de gola alta com o Punk-02 no peito esquerdo, jaqueta roxa com capuz e mangas largas, botas DOM roxas, luvas pretas e capacete vermelho de aviadora — não vestido laranja, correção que o cânone impõe. Usa o pronome arcaico masculino 'washi' e fala em estilo antiquado. Rouba viajantes e piratas que passam por Egghead e justifica isso a Shaka como necessário para manter a pesquisa de Vegapunk funcionando; não teve escrúpulo em cogitar entregar os Chapéus de Palha à CP0 sabendo que Lucci é assassino. Sua natureza 'má' fez o próprio Stella suspeitar injustamente dela pelo roubo da Mother Flame, até seu nome ser limpo. É impulsiva, combativa e odeia receber ordens — sobretudo de Shaka —, mas tem lucidez para recuar quando não pode vencer. Apesar do rótulo, tem lado compaixão: quis lutar contra a CP0 para vingar Atlas, ficou aflita quando Edison quase morreu e chorou pelos Satélites, secando as lágrimas depressa ao lembrar que todos continuam 'vivos'. Herdou o gênio médico de Vegapunk: confia em ressuscitá-lo por clonagem e restaurar as funções cognitivas de Kuma, mas precisa de laboratório — por isso planeja construir um novo em Elbaf, onde virou guardiã de Bonney. Pilota o mecha Vegaforce-01, capaz de carregar o Thousand Sunny nas mãos, e comanda Feras do Mar ciborgues cujo instinto orgânico ela nunca conseguiu sobrescrever por completo. Sua arma é a Bubble Gun, que dispara bolhas de energia do mar capazes de desviar ataques e enfraquecer usuários de Akuma no Mi.

**Lilith**, also designated as **Punk-02** (*Panku-Tsū*<sup>?</sup>), is one of six satellites of Dr. Vegapunk, embodying his aspect of "evil".<sup>[3]</sup>

She is a major ally of the Straw Hat Pirates during the Egghead and Elbaph Arcs, and ultimately the only Vegapunk to escape with them after the Egghead Incident.<sup>[4]</sup>

### Appearance[]

Lilith has the appearance of a tall young woman with wild ginger hair and purple eyes, with her hair covering her right eye. She wears a pink, high-collared, full-body jumpsuit that bears her designation of Punk-02 on the left breast; she also wears a purple hooded jacket with sleeves that are baggy around the forearm area, purple DOM shoes, black gloves, and a red aviator helmet.<sup>[1]</sup> Like the other satellites, she wears an earpiece and antenna on her left ear which enables communication with Vegapunk's other manifestations.<sup>[3]</sup> In the One Piece Anime, she wears a black hooded jacket during the Elbaph Arc.

### Personality[]

Being designated the "evil" satellite of Vegapunk, Lilith is the manifestation of Vegapunk's ruthless and malevolent traits, thus coming off as a very deceptive and untrustworthy individual. She utilizes her technology and weaponry to steal from voyagers, chiefly pirates, who trespass on Egghead, justifying this to Shaka as necessary for the sake of keeping Vegapunk's research operational.<sup>[3]</sup> She also thought nothing of handing the Straw Hat Pirates to CP0, being fully aware that Rob Lucci was an assassin.<sup>[5]</sup> Her "evil" nature made the original Vegapunk suspect her of betraying them until her name was cleared.<sup>[6]</sup>

Lilith possesses a rash attitude and often acts impulsively, annoyed, and derisively. For this reason, she is one of the most outgoingly combative and daring of the satellites, being eager to confront any perceived enemies directly and violently, although she has moments of clarity and will pull back in situations she cannot win.<sup>[3]</sup> Lilith also hates being ordered around, especially by Shaka, her "good" counterpart whose exemplary behavior tends to constantly get under her skin.

Despite being "evil," Lilith actually has a compassionate side. She cares about her fellow satellites, as she wanted to fight CP0 to avenge Atlas. She was also deeply worried when Edison almost got killed by S-Shark's laser and relieved when Sanji saved him in time. She mourned the deaths of Stella and her fellow satellites, although she quickly got over it after crying for a bit, knowing that all the other satellites could still be “alive.”

She uses the first-person pronoun *washi* (わし<sup>?</sup>), which is an old-fashioned term usually associated with men in period fiction, such as samurai. She also uses stereotypical old-fashioned speech patterns such as the copula *ja* (じゃ<sup>?</sup>).

### Relationships[]

#### Satellites[]

While Vegapunk's satellites all refer to themselves as Vegapunk, they each exemplify only one portion of Vegapunk's personality and all have their own wills and desires and are therefore effectively six unique people. While this normally isn't an issue, as Vegapunk is supposed to synchronize his brain with all of his satellites via Punk Records, York decided to betray Vegapunk and her fellow satellites in an effort to become a World Noble. Once her treachery was revealed, she was effectively treated as an enemy by Vegapunk and the other satellites. After escaping Egghead, Lilith revealed her fellow Satellite are technically alive.

##### Shaka[]

She and Shaka have a rivalry toward each other due to being the "Evil" and "Good" Vegapunks, respectively. However, Lilith cared for Shaka, mourning his "death" due to York's betrayal.<sup>[7]</sup>

##### Edison[]

Lilith cares for Edison, as seen when she mourned for his "death" after York's betrayal even though he was merely injured.

##### Pythagoras[]

Lilith cared for Pythagoras, mourning his "death" due to York's betrayal.

##### York[]

Once York's betrayal was revealed, Lilith became furious at her, referring to her as a traitor.

#### Straw Hat Pirates[]

Initially, Lilith didn't care about the infamous pirate crew and only 'saved' them from drowning so she could plunder them. While she agreed to bring them to Egghead under Shaka's instructions and was happy to explain the technology of the island, she remained hostile to them. But once she learned the World Government had come to assassinate her and the other Vegapunks, she accepted them as allies. She became more friendly with them and worked alongside them against the Seraphim and later Jaygarcia Saturn. Despite being the only Vegapunk they managed to save, Lilith didn't blame them and told them they hadn't failed because they saved her. When she saw that Luffy remained depressed over his perceived failure, she further attempted to reassure him that everyone had actually survived, despite appearances.

#### Jewelry Bonney[]

Lilith has a positive relationship with Bonney after they left Egghead together with the Straw Hat Pirates and the Giant Warrior Pirates. She has taken on the role of Bonney's guardian.<sup>[8]</sup> Upon landing on Elbaph, the two decided to live there.

### Abilities and Powers[]

Being one of six manifestations of the genius scientist Vegapunk, Lilith has contributed to his numerous technological advances and breakthroughs. Despite the technology and weaponry at her disposal, Lilith was cautioned by Shaka that she was not equipped to take on Roronoa Zoro and Nico Robin, two members of an Emperor's crew with bounties around 1 billion, in combat.<sup>[3]</sup>

#### Medical Expertise[]

As one of Vegapunk's satellites, Lilith has inherited the latter's medical genius, with her being confident in being able to revive Vegapunk through a cloning method, as well as restore Bartholomew Kuma's cognitive functions. However, she admitted that such feats would require extensive laboratory equipment to achieve, as she plans to build a new lab on Elbaph due to Egghead's scientific laboratories and equipment having been either destroyed by the Buster Call or reclaimed by the government along with York.

#### Engineering Expertise[]

Lilith has made heavy use of mechas and cybernetically enhanced animals to aid her in combat. She pilots a giant mecha called the Vegaforce-01, which is extremely large to the point of being able to carry the Thousand Sunny in its hands. The Vega Force is able to move underwater and unleash explosions when it punches. Additionally, Lilith has power over Sea Beasts that she has turned into cyborgs. These Sea Beasts are equipped with numerous weapons, with an example being the Mecha-Shark having a torpedo launcher in its mouth. This army of cyborg creatures allows Lilith to easily surround and overwhelm ships sailing the sea, enabling her to loot them. However, she has had issues with achieving programming to completely override the organic urges of the creatures, which limits her ability to have them do exactly what she wants; Shaka himself would also note that any equipment, weaponry, and modified Sea Beasts at Lilith's disposal would be useless against a swordsman of Zoro's caliber.<sup>[1]</sup><sup>[3]</sup>

#### Weapons[]

Lilith wields a large futuristic-looking gun,<sup>[9]</sup> called a **Bubble Gun** (バブルガン, *Baburugan*<sup>?</sup>) because it shoots bubbles that are capable of deflecting any attacks.<sup>[10]</sup> These bubbles, called **Bubble Balls** (バブルボール, *Baburubōru*<sup>?</sup>),<sup>[11]</sup> are made of a special material containing sea energy, meaning they also serve as a weakness for Devil Fruit users.<sup>[12]</sup>

#### Final Saga[]

##### Egghead Arc[]

When the Straw Hat Pirates drew near to Egghead on the Thousand Sunny, the Mecha-Shark went to attack them. With the shark's primal urges overriding its programming and threatening to completely annihilate the crew's possessions, Lilith went underwater in the Vegaforce-01 and took it down before lifting the Sunny back above water. Lilith then exited the Vega Force and introduced herself as Dr. Vegapunk, to general confusion.<sup>[1]</sup> Lilith informed the pirates that she did not rescue them but rather spared them from the Mecha-Shark so she could rob them of their valuables. She summoned her army of weaponized Sea Beasts to surround the Straw Hats, ordering them to surrender to her their valued possessions at once, but then received a call from fellow satellite Shaka, who informed her she had blundered in her antagonism of the pirates, pointing out that they were poised to target and overwhelm her if they retaliate, much to Lilith's dismay, embarrassed that she overlooked something that could have easily led to her downfall. Roronoa Zoro told Lilith to play nice, and Shaka told his counterpart to bring the pirates to him, as he has interest in the Straw Hats.<sup>[3]</sup>

Though not very pleased about this slipped opportunity, Lilith complied with Shaka's request and used Vegaforce-01 to transport the Sunny towards Egghead into the upper part of the island, the research laboratory.<sup>[14]</sup> She then led the Straw Hats, minus Brook and Zoro, into the lab while explaining its technological specialties. She did this, however, purposefully, as she made contact with Edison, and she, alongside Edison and Pythagoras, unleashed the Seraphim S-Shark onto the Straw Hats to test its fighting capabilities and qualities. However, their test run was soon ended by Shaka, displeasing especially Lilith, who was furious at his second interference in her meddlings.<sup>[15]</sup>

When Pythagoras informed Shaka that a World Government ship with CP0 onboard was arriving at Egghead, Lilith suggested handing the pirates over to Rob Lucci.<sup>[5]</sup> However, when Lucci suspiciously pointed out several World Government ships went missing lately, Lilith annoyingly and impulsively told the group to dock at Egghead and see for themselves, ignoring Pythagoras, who was against her impulsiveness.<sup>[16]</sup> When both the CP0 squad and the Seraphim effectively landed on Egghead, Lilith was excited at the prospect of fighting them, wanting to avenge Atlas, who got damaged previously by Lucci. She then mentioned taking everyone to the port with Vegaforce-01 after Vegapunk's secret ally confirmed their support,<sup>[9]</sup> and when both CP0 and the Seraphim landed on the Labophase, Lilith made her way out alongside Edison to issue a personal command to stop the Seraphim from attacking the Labophase,<sup>[17]</sup> later being able to successfully make her way outside, but she is then targeted by S-Hawk before she can issue a command. However, the attack is blocked by Zoro, and everybody is saved by Edison, who commands the Seraphim to stop.<sup>[18]</sup>

Lilith later met up with everybody inside the Laboratory when the main Vegapunk body, Stella, went missing. She paired up with Usopp, Franky, and York, searching inside Tower C. Inside the tower, the group found Pythagoras' body without his head, having been separated by a previous explosion. Lilith grew impatient with Pythagoras when he freaked out about who attacked him before she turned to York, who was turned into stone by S-Snake. Lilith tried to stop her; however, she had to jump off the bridge alongside Pythagoras, Franky, and Usopp when they were shot by S-Snake. She then brushed off Franky's suggestion to directly fight the Seraphim after effortlessly trying to contact Shaka, stating they could not beat S-Snake.<sup>[19]</sup> Lilith later pulled out her bubble gun, revealing a significant weakness the Seraphim have that her bubble gum could exploit: sea energy. 

However, her bubbles barely missed S-Snake, who seducingly tricked Franky into releasing a bubble he held onto for her and then used her Devil Fruit powers to petrify both Lilith and Usopp.<sup>[20]</sup><sup>[13]</sup> The petrification was later lifted as Lilith was seen having joined the other surviving satellites, Vegapunk and the Straw Hats, after they had restrained the traitor York. In order to help their escape from the Marines that had surrounded Egghead, Lilith came along with Luffy, Franky, and Jewelry Bonney to both the Thousand Sunny and Vegaforce-01, with Vegapunk having ordered Lilith specifically to pilot Vegaforce-01.<sup>[21]</sup> When the Vegaforce-01 was destroyed,<sup>[22]</sup> Franky had Lilith pilot his General Franky instead.<sup>[23]</sup>

Back on the Thousand Sunny, Brook devised a plan to use his Devil Fruit ability to freeze the Island Clouds and glide back across to the rear entrance. While Lilith was initially pleased with and praised the plan, she questioned how he would stop the ship at the end, to which Brook had no answer, and she slapped him and worried they would slide off the island.<sup>[24]</sup> When Brook was called by Nami again and stated how the ship was still sliding through the rear entrance, Lilith slapped him again, stating they would hit the barrier if the ship did not stop. She then got in touch with Nami to tell her something.<sup>[25]</sup> The Thousand Sunny was then caught before it slid off the island due to Usopp's **Midori Boshi: Take Jave Rin**, saving Lilith and Brook. While saved and regrouped with Usopp, Lilith was still annoyed at Brook and kicked him.<sup>[26]</sup> When Jaygarcia Saturn reached the group's location and attacked Robin, Lilith joined the Straw Hats to fight him off, shooting the elder to no avail. After he brushed them aside, Lilith and the others noticed the clouds extending outward.<sup>[27]</sup> 

Lilith noted that while they may be able to land in the sea, they would still be surrounded by the enemy ships stationed there. Ethanbaron V. Nusjuro then caught up to the ship's location to Lilith's and the others' horror but was countered from attacking due to Zoro and Jinbe.<sup>[28]</sup> Eventually, Zoro and Jinbe made their way back onto the ship, although Nusjuro remained in the way of escape. Lilith denied all requests to leave at that moment, citing the fact that any interference by Nusjuro would ruin their trajectory and likely make them fall to their deaths. Before she could continue, Atlas suddenly punched her into the ground and then snapped a switch behind her head. This resulted in Lilith being forcefully disconnected from Punk Records, leading York to believe she was deceased. Atlas then used the moment to sacrifice herself so that the crew could escape, taking the unconscious Lilith with them.<sup>[29]</sup>

Sometime later, Lilith awoke aboard the Great Eirik. She did not take long to recognize the environment and was met with apologies from the various Straw Hats who were watching over her about their apparent failure to save the other Vegapunks. Lilith stated she had already known about the circumstances, to their confusion. Lilith promptly burst into tears from the news but dried them just as quickly as she realized she now felt hunger, a new feeling associated with her being disconnected from York. Lilith was led to meet with Luffy, who was under the impression that he had failed Stella and was eating massive amounts of food while crying. Lilith was annoyed by this but relented and moved to explain that Luffy had in fact not failed at all. She explained that the other Vegapunks were only "dead", at least physically, and that they were still technically alive, to everyone's shock. The news was taken well by Luffy, who promptly perked back up and met with his giant friends, who restarted the earlier party they had had to cancel. Lilith joined in on the party with her new friends, celebrating their success.<sup>[30]</sup>

##### Elbaph Arc[]

After the party, Lilith noticed that six of the Straw Hats were missing and joined the other Straw Hats' efforts to find them but was unsuccessful.<sup>[31]</sup> She later listened to Robin reading from the newspaper about the Egghead Incident as well as the giants' story about the "Accursed Prince" Loki. Soon after that, the group reached Elbaph together with the Giant Warrior Pirates.<sup>[32]</sup> Lilith, together with Bonney, beamingly watched the rainbow created by the Giant Warrior Pirates with the help of a painter and listened to Dorry and Brogy explaining about the three levels of Elbaph as the group then finally made it to Dorry and Brogy's home village as they also finally reunited with Luffy's group there.<sup>[33]</sup> Lilith then marveled at the technological wonders the giants had combined when Dorry and Brogy explained that Luffy's group had arrived with a "Svarr." She later accompanied the group when Robin decided to reunite with Saul.<sup>[34]</sup>

After the reunion, Lilith formally introduced herself to Saul as "Vegapunk", leaving Saul confused, but she assured him she would explain to him later why Vegapunk was a female. Then, she asked Jinbe to fetch her the box he saved from Egghead, as she explained it contained a perfect clone of Vegapunk from the time when he visited Elbaph 20 years ago and that Vegapunk created this clone so the World Government would not notice him when he left his laboratory, thus reducing suspicions about Vegapunk himself. She then revealed her plans of staying at Elbaph and creating a new laboratory, and upon Bonney's question if she would cure Kuma, Lilith informed her that she planned on doing so.<sup>[35]</sup>

Following a few shenanigans involving the Straw Hats within the Owl Library, Lilith departed from the main group with Bonney, the pair intent on making themselves a home on Elbaph.<sup>[36]</sup> Much later, the two managed to get themselves lost in the Sun World but, at the same time, located a strange laboratory built into a tree. They eventually met up with Ripley, who had been giving Franky a tour of Elbaph's Treasure Tree Adam. While conversing with their friends, Lilith revealed her estimations of the laboratory's age to be at least 3,000 years, which shocked Franky. Lilith then declared her intention of settling down in the lab but was interrupted from further talking upon the arrival of a storm MMA.<sup>[37]</sup>

Fortunately, the group was able to escape with Ripley, giving Franky the opportunity to shoot down the MMA.<sup>[38]</sup> In a stroke of luck, the group happened upon the Giant Warrior Pirates, who had just crashed into the forest. Lilith remained silent as Shepherd Sommers revealed over a broadcast that the Knights of God had taken Elbaph's children hostage.<sup>[39]</sup> The price Elbaph had to pay for their safety was the destruction of the Owl Library and the Walrus School. A while later, the children were eventually rescued, but both buildings continued to burn after being set ablaze by Rimoshifu Killingham. Wanting to assist, Lilith revealed a few devices called the Omni-Drain Converters and mentioned that they could be charged using all the latent energy present around them. To prove her point, Lilith used the ODCs to completely vanquish the fires, freezing both locations. Afterward, she used one to charge up a bottle of Cola, dubbing it Super Vega-Cola as she passed it to Franky. Lilith explained that Franky's design was underutilized and that the new beverage she created would allow him to power himself up far more than he could before.<sup>[40]</sup>

Afterward, Lilith assisted in putting out more fires using the ODCs. The scale of the fires distressed her, as the ODCs struggled to extinguish them.<sup>[41]</sup> Although the Straw Hats managed to decisively defeat the Knights of God, this only earned them the personal ire of Nerona Imu, who decided to personally tend to Elbaph. His arrival was swift and chaotic, with his first actions being to free both Sommers and Killingham from their bindings. In doing so, he incapacitated many of the strongest fighters present, including Sanji. Bonney and Lilith managed to rescue him while he was down, taking Sanji to a safe location.<sup>[42]</sup> Bonney tried to spectate the fight between Imu, Loki, and Ragnir, prompting a scolding from Lilith, who reminded Bonney that it was now her responsibility to look after her. Lilith then contemplated Imu's identity, noting his clear dominance over the Knights despite not being one of the Elders, and considered that the World Government must have quite a few monsters, given its 800-year history. Before she could think further, Sanji stirred and immediately took off to fight again, declaring it his duty to save Elbaph and its giant women.<sup>[43]</sup>

### Trivia[]

- Lilith's name may be based on Lilith is the name of a figure in Mesopotamian and Judaic mythology, with accounts and interpretations generally depicting her as a primordial demon, which reflects her embodiment of Dr. Vegapunk's "evil".
